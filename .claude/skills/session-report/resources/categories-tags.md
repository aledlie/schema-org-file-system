## Category Selection Guidelines

1. **Primary category first**: The main focus of the work
2. **2-3 categories max**: Keep focused
3. **Use kebab-case**: `performance-optimization` not `Performance Optimization`

---

## Tag Selection Guidelines

1. **4-8 tags**: Enough for searchability, not overwhelming
2. **Specific technologies**: `typescript` not `programming`
3. **Project names**: `ast-grep-mcp`, `sentry`
4. **Concepts**: `caching`, `optimization`, `testing`
5. **Use kebab-case**: Consistent formatting
