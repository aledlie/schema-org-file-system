## No-Annual-Fee Starter Stack

For someone building credit or minimizing fees:
1. Chase Freedom Unlimited — 1.5x catch-all + 3x dining/drugstore
2. Citi Custom Cash — 5% on one category (groceries OR gas OR dining)
3. Discover it / Chase Freedom Flex — rotating 5% categories

## Premium Travel Stack (if fees justified by benefits)

1. Amex Gold — dining + groceries (4x)
2. Chase Sapphire Reserve — travel + Priority Pass + 1.5cpp redemption floor
3. Amex Blue Cash Preferred — 6% groceries if not using MR ecosystem for that spend
4. Chase Ink Biz Cash — 5x internet/phone (small business or freelance)
