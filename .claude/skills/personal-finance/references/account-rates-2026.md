## I-Bonds (Series I, TreasuryDirect)

- Composite rate May–October 2026: **4.26%** (0.90% fixed + 3.34% variable)
- Fixed rate (permanent for bond's life): 0.90%
- Purchase limit: $10,000/person/year (+ $5,000 via tax refund)
- Minimum hold: 12 months (cannot redeem earlier)
- Penalty if redeemed < 5 years: forfeit last 3 months of interest
